# BFR — Big Fucking Rocket

Arena shooter — fly the BFR through asteroids, meteors, and shooting stars. Doge pickups. Super Heavy hot-stage intro. Occupy Mars.

## Play

```powershell
.\serve.ps1
```

Then open http://localhost:8088/

## Controls

Fly around on the title screen with no enemies. Hit **Launch** (or Enter) when you want the belt to come at you.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` / `↑` | Thrust |
| `A` `D` / `←` `→` | Rotate |
| `S` / `↓` | Galactic dash — instant forward boost |
| `Space` / `F` / `J` | Fire (hold) |
| `Shift` / `X` | Hyper teleport |
| `Enter` | Launch (start waves) |
| `P` | Pause |
| Phone | Tilt to fly, hold to fire |

The arena wraps. Lead shots. Dash out of a swarm.

## Weapons

Unlocked by destroying original invaders (the split-offs do not count):

1. **Pulse** — heavy bolts
2. **Twin** — dual streams
3. **Rail** — charge beam, pierces everything
4. **Spread** — five-way cone
5. **Seekers** — heat-seeking blue and gold

## Drops

- Hearts
- Score orbs
- **Overclock** — fire rate (stacks until you take a hit)
- **Nova** — chain lightning
- **Titan** — heavier rounds
- Lightning bolt — score multiplier + overdrive

## Waves

The Belt → Meteor Storm → Starfall → **The Impactor**

Asteroids split. Meteors dive. Shooting stars rip across the sky. Power-ups are tiny Doge faces. Pick Mars, Earth, or Moon on the title screen.
